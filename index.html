The Problem

Most building gyms never get used.

01

Quality equipment, zero guidance

Residents show up motivated but have no organized way to maximize the facility. Without direction, newcomers can feel lost and stop coming. A room full of machines is not a fitness amenity.

02

No accountability, no results

Without structure and follow-through, residents stop before they start. The gym sees less engagement and the amenity quietly loses its value to the building.

03

A clear separator from buildings with gyms

Prospective residents might ask what makes your building different. A personalized fitness app tied to actual programming modeled after the exact equipment in your gym is a real answer.

04

One-size-fits-all doesn't fit anyone

A beginner and a former athlete need completely different programs. AmenityFit adjusts automatically with no added equipment or manual input required from building staff.

Questions

Straight answers.

What does it cost?

Billed per building by unit count, with no per-resident fees and no usage caps: up to 50 units $599 a month, 51 to 100 units $999, 101 to 200 units $1,499, 201 to 300 units $1,999, 301 to 500 units $2,799, 501 to 1,000 units $3,999. Over 1,000 units is a custom quote. Paying for the year up front takes 10% off.

What do residents pay?

Nothing. AmenityFit is included as a building amenity. Residents never pay, and the building does not charge them a separate fee for it.

How long is the agreement?

The initial term is 12 months from the service start date. It then renews a year at a time unless either side gives written notice at least 60 days before the term ends. Paying monthly is a payment schedule, not a shorter commitment.

Are the terms negotiable?

No. Pricing and terms are the same for every building, which keeps it simple and fair. Portfolios get volume discounts set out in advance: 10% for 5 to 9 buildings, 13% for 10 to 19, and 17% for 20 to 39.

How do we pay?

By invoice through Stripe, due within 15 days of the invoice date, monthly or the full year up front for 10% off.

Who sets it up, and how long does it take?

We do. Once the agreement is signed and the first invoice is paid, your building is set up and your team receives the manager login and your residents' invite codes by email. You tell us what equipment your gym has, and every program is built around it.

Is resident data private?

Your dashboard and monthly report show building-level adoption and engagement. Personal health and fitness details stay private to each resident and are not shared with the building.

Do you work with hotels and portfolios?

Yes. Hotels have their own plans by room count, and portfolios of buildings or hotels are covered under one agreement with volume discounts.
