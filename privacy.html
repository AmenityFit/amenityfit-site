Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026  ·  Fitmakesenz LLC

Fitmakesenz LLC ("AmenityFit," "we," "us," or "our") operates AmenityFit, including the app residents use (on iPhone and at app.amenityfit.app) and the website at amenityfit.app (together, the "Service"). AmenityFit is offered to residential buildings and partner properties as an amenity; residents join with an invite code from their building.

This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to it. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us:

  • Account details: name, email address, password, building invite code, and profile photo if you add one
  • Profile: age, gender, height, weight, fitness goals, experience level, equipment preferences, and any injuries or limitations you share
  • Training: workouts, sets and weights you log, sessions completed, streaks, personal bests, goals and plans you add to your calendar, and effort ratings
  • Nutrition: calorie and macro targets, foods you log (typed or spoken), allergies and foods you avoid or prefer, meal plans you choose, grocery lists, and your weekly weigh-ins
  • Conversations with the Coach (the Fitness Assistant), typed or spoken, and short notes you ask it to remember (for example, an injury or a preference)
  • Training partners: people you choose to connect with for shared sessions and challenges
  • Applications and inquiries submitted through amenityfit.app

Information collected when you use the Service:

  • Location during an activity you start (such as a run, walk, ride or hike), to record your route, distance and pace; location is used only while the app is in use
  • Your approximate location for local weather, so the Coach and your calendar can suggest what fits the conditions
  • Health and fitness data from Apple Health and connected wearables (such as Apple Watch or Whoop) when you choose to connect them: workouts, heart rate, active calories and similar measures
  • Device and usage information: device type, app version, time zone, notification settings, and basic usage and error data that helps us keep the Service working

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Build and adjust your training program, goals and plans
  • Power the Coach, so its answers fit your training, schedule, body, nutrition and preferences
  • Track your progress and show it back to you, including share cards you choose to create
  • Run the nutrition features: your targets, what you have left for the day, meal and week plans, grocery lists and weigh-in trends
  • Send reminders and notifications you turn on
  • Give building managers anonymous, building-level totals (see section 4)
  • Keep the Service secure, fix problems, and improve it using patterns that cannot identify you

We do not use your personal information for advertising, and we never sell it.

3. Health, Nutrition and Safety

Your weight, food log, allergies, targets and health data are visible only to you and are used only to provide the Service. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with your building.

Calorie and nutrition numbers are estimates to guide you. Allergies and foods you avoid are checked against meal plans and recipes, but always check ingredients and labels yourself.

For anyone under 18, the Coach does not set calorie targets, calorie deficits or weigh-ins; it focuses on eating enough and eating well for training and growth. If a message suggests someone may be struggling with food (such as restricting, purging or bingeing), the Coach gives no numbers or meal plans, responds with care and shares support resources. We do not record or store any diagnosis.

4. What Building Managers See

Building managers see only anonymous, building-level totals, such as how many residents are active, total workouts completed and weekly activity rates. They never see your name, your workouts, your body measurements, your nutrition, your conversations with the Coach or any other individual data.

5. The Coach (AI)

The Coach is powered by Anthropic's Claude. When you message the Coach, your message and the parts of your profile, training and nutrition it needs to answer are sent to Anthropic to generate the reply, under Anthropic's commercial terms, which do not permit it to use this data to train its models. Spoken questions are turned into text by your phone's speech recognition before they are sent.

If you turn on spoken replies, the Coach's reply text (not your account details) is sent to our voice provider to be read aloud.

We save your Coach conversation and the notes you ask it to remember to your account, so the Coach can pick up where you left off. Please do not share medical records, financial details or passwords with the Coach. The Coach is not for emergencies: if you have an emergency, call 911.

6. Training Partners

When you connect with a training partner, you both see the shared sessions and challenges you take part in together, such as distance, pace and scores. You can remove a partner at any time.

7. Who We Share Information With

We share information only with service providers that run parts of the Service for us, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database, storage and servers)
  • Anthropic (the Coach)
  • Inworld and ElevenLabs (reading the Coach's replies aloud, when you use that feature)
  • Apple (Apple Health, WeatherKit for weather, notifications and speech recognition)
  • Vercel (hosting the app), Vimeo (exercise videos), Cloudinary (images) and Esri (maps and place names)

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

8. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile), or by emailing us; we delete your personal data within 30 days. If your building ends its AmenityFit subscription, we keep your data for up to 90 days, in case access is restored, and then delete it unless you ask us to delete it sooner. Totals that cannot identify you may be kept.

9. Security

We protect your information with industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit and access controls on our servers. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Ages 16 and 17, and Children

You must be at least 16 to use AmenityFit. For users aged 16 and 17, we collect only what the Service needs, never use it for advertising and never sell it, and the safety rules in section 3 apply. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16; if we learn that we have, we delete it. If you believe a child under 16 has used the Service, contact us.

11. Your Choices and Rights

  • Update your profile, targets, allergies and preferences at any time in the app
  • Turn off location, notifications, Apple Health or wearable access in the app or in your phone's settings
  • Hide calorie numbers, remove logged foods, and remove training partners
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. To use any of these rights, email support@fitmakesenz.com.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we change the date at the top, and for material changes we will tell you in the app. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@fitmakesenz.com, Fitmakesenz LLC.
