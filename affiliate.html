Your own referral link

A unique code tied to you. When you refer a building, make sure they mention your code when they apply. That's what officially credits the referral to you. If a building applies without it, we have no way to know it came from you, and it's treated as an unprompted signup with no commission owed.

A dashboard that tracks everything

The details

  • 15% of monthly contract value, paid for 12 months from the date each building signs. Staggered independently per building, not on one shared clock.
  • Single tier only. You earn on buildings you personally refer, never on other affiliates you bring into the program. No pyramid structure, ever.
  • Paid in USD via automated payout, regardless of where you're located.
  • Open internationally. Property managers, cold callers, and industry connections anywhere are welcome to apply.
  • Guaranteed for the full term. Once a building signs, your 12-month payout is secured even if that building later cancels early.
  • Disclosure required. When you refer a building, you're required to let them know you may earn a commission if they sign, in line with FTC guidelines. We'll give you simple, pre-approved language to use so this is never awkward.
